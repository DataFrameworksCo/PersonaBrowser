Why Persona Browser

Privacy built into
every tab.

Not just incognito mode. True session isolation so your accounts, trackers, and history never mix.

🛡️
Session Isolation

Each persona gets its own cookies, local storage, and session cache. Zero cross-contamination between identities.

🚫
Tracker Blocking

Built-in blocking of Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, and dozens of other known trackers — no extension needed.

🎨
5 Themes

Dark, Light, Midnight, Forest, and Ocean. Pick your vibe and accent color from 10 options.

🧩
Sidebar Widgets

Clock, Notes, Bookmarks, and Weather right in your sidebar. The browser becomes your workspace.

🔒
HTTPS Enforcement

Optional HTTPS-only mode ensures you never accidentally browse over an insecure connection.

🌐
Search Engine Choice

DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Google, or Bing. You choose — and it applies globally, not per tab.
